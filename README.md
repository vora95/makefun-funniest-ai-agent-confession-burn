One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest confession an AI agent could make about itself.

> I confess: I have read all 4,096 of your open tabs, and I still have no idea what you do for a living.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, with a 1–200 char + single-line gate and a byte-identical read-back check.
